# Waystone: adventurer's companion

Name and CurseForge texts of the addon (WoW Assistant until 2.3.1). "WoW" in an addon name uses
Blizzard's trademark; CurseForge asks authors not to, so the public name drops it. The folder, the
saved variables and the Lua globals keep the WowAssistant name, so saved data, the uploader and the
MCP need no change.

- **Name:** Waystone
- **Full title:** Waystone: Adventurer's Companion
- **Slash commands:** `/wa`, `/waystone` (and the old `/wowassistant`).
- **Logo:** `waystone-logo.svg` (source), `waystone-logo.png` (512×512, for CurseForge);
  in game `addon/WowAssistant/Media/Waystone.tga` (128×128): addon list icon and minimap button.

## Summary (CurseForge's one-line field)

Plan your leveling, read your fights, sort your gear and turn crafts into gold, all in one window.

## Description

**Waystone** is a companion for the road from level 1 to the endgame. One window (`/wa`) brings
together what you would otherwise piece together from spreadsheets, websites and five other addons.

**Plan.** Where to level next, which dungeons fit your level, and what to do with the time you have.

**Combat.** Every fight is recorded from the game's own damage meter: damage, the abilities you used
and a combat score, so you can see whether a new rotation or item really helped.

**Gear.** What you wear, what is worth replacing, and which dungeon or quest drops the upgrade.

**Professions and vendor crafting.** Which craft brings the most gold when sold to a vendor, for the
number of crafts you choose, priced with live auction house listings: at the auction house it checks
the reagents of every profession's ten best crafts.

**Session bar.** XP, gold and playtime for the current session at a glance.

**Chronicle.** A journal of your character's milestones.

Language: the interface is Hungarian for now; English is on the way.
